# Changelog

Each `## vX.Y.Z` section becomes the GitHub release notes and is shown in the
app's "Update available" pop-up.

## v1.0.0

First public release.

### Library
- Download songs for offline listening, on Wi-Fi only or on mobile data too
- Sync with your PC library by scanning a QR code
- Playlists and Liked songs, with liked songs downloaded automatically

### Player
- Lock-screen and notification controls
- Sleep timer: minutes or end of the current song
- Super shuffle across every song in your playlists
- Home-screen "Now playing" widget with play/pause and next

### Stats
- Listening stats: time listened, most played songs and a song of the day

### Maintenance
- Update yt-dlp from Settings when songs stop loading
- In-app update check with changelog
