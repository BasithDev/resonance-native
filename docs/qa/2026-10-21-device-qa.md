# Resonance v1.0.0 — device QA (Motorola Edge 60 Fusion, Android 17), 2026-10-21

## Bugs
1. [Critical] Bundled yt-dlp (2025.11.12) is rejected by YouTube (HTTP 403). Streaming waits ~10-15 s then skips to the next song; downloads end in "Retry". Fixed only by Settings > yt-dlp > Update (now 2026.08.19). Reproduced on the PC with the exact bundled file; current yt-dlp works under the same conditions. The app never updates yt-dlp by itself.
2. [Major] Queue sheet cannot scroll. With a long queue (e.g. Play offline = 97 songs) only the first ~13 "Up next" songs are reachable. Likely cause: ScrollView in QueueSheet not height-constrained, or row gestures swallow vertical drags.
3. [Minor] Play counts include failed/instant plays: "Yaaro Yaaro — 2 plays · 9s", "God Mode Begins — 4 plays · 1m" (plays from the 403 period count).
4. [Minor] Android media session reports a wrong position (0:33 while the app shows 4:25 at end of song) — notification/lock-screen progress may be wrong.
5. [Intermittent, not reproduced] Playback once stopped by itself (paused at 0:00 on the current song with a song still queued), right after removing the download of the queued next song while it re-downloaded. Repeating the steps advanced normally.

## UX notes
- Search results only offer Save (+). Downloading needs Library > ⋮ > Download to phone — easy to miss (the owner thought downloading was broken).
- Search ✓ (saved) button keeps the accessibility label "Save to library".
- Failed download of a video deleted from YouTube only says "download failed"; "Retry download" can never work. Should say the video is unavailable.
- Settings "Download on mobile data": hint always reads "Off: downloads wait for Wi-Fi", even when on.
- Playlist ••• menu, delete-playlist and remove-song confirmations use the stock Android dialog (grey, serif, teal buttons), not the app's design.
- Scrolled content (e.g. Settings) slides under the status bar, overlapping the clock/icons.
- Playlist "Play" ignores an active filter (plays the whole playlist).

## Works
- Launch; Library (greeting, stats chip, chips All/Playlists/Liked, Play all, playlists carousel with download counts, All songs).
- Search on YouTube (~5 s); streaming a non-downloaded song; Save to library; ⋮ > Download to phone (< 20 s, while playing).
- Full player: play/pause, next, previous (restart after 3 s, back otherwise), like, shuffle, repeat all/one/off, waveform seek, close (⌄ and Back).
- Liking a search-only song saves it and auto-downloads it; Liked songs count updates.
- Queue: Up next, drag reorder, swipe left to remove, Clear, Play next, Add to queue; auto-advance to downloaded and streamed songs.
- Sleep timer menu; "End of song" stops at the end and clears the chip.
- Notification media controls: next, pause, play, previous.
- Super shuffle: all playlist songs once (14 = 8 + 4 + 2, no duplicates).
- Playlists: create, add songs (toast + cover update), filter, Play, download all, rename, delete (songs kept).
- Song menu: Remove download (keep in library), re-download, Remove from library (with confirmation).
- Offline tab: storage summary, download queue progress, Play offline, Shuffle.
- Stats screen: today/week/month/year/all-time, song of the day, most played.
- Settings: toggles, storage, yt-dlp Update ("Already up to date"), Check for updates ("You're up to date").
- Sync screen opens the camera scanner with instructions; widget provider is registered.

## Not tested (need the owner)
- QR sync end to end (camera must see the laptop QR).
- Offline / airplane mode (turning Wi-Fi off cuts the wireless debug link).
- Lock-screen controls and sleep timer with the screen off (phone can't be unlocked remotely).
- Home-screen widget (must be placed by hand).
- In-app "Update available" pop-up (needs a newer release than 1.0.0).
